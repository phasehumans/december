today i watched a video by varun mayya about belief. the idea is simple. if you really believe you will do something, and you put in the work, then ten years later you will probably have done it. belief is the first step to becoming that person.

i have been thinking about it all day.

## the wannabe

everyone makes fun of the wannabe. the kid who calls himself a founder before he has a company. the person who says "i am a writer" after three blog posts. fake it until you make it sounds like a polite way to say lying.

i do not think it is lying. you are not lying to other people about what you have done. you are acting like the person you want to be, before you are that person.

## belief changes what you do

belief does not do the work. it makes you show up for the work.

if you believe you will be a writer, you write on a tuesday night when nobody asked you to. if you do not believe it, why would you? if you believe you will build a company, you read the boring stuff about taxes and hiring, because one day you will need it.

nobody can see the belief. they can only see what you do because of it. and what you do, every day for years, is what you become.

## the odds

there is no guarantee. you can believe in something for ten years, do the work, and still not get there. that happens.

but look at the other person. the one who does not believe, so does not try. their odds are zero. yours are not. as long as you keep making things, you have better odds than everyone who is only thinking about it.

your belief does not need to be true today. it needs to be useful today.

## the wannabe stops being one

here is what happens to the wannabe who keeps going. they do the thing badly. then a bit less badly. then one day someone calls them a writer, or a founder, and they did not say it first.

that is the day they stop being a wannabe. not because they faked it well. because they kept doing it for so long that it stopped being fake.

so decide who you want to be. then act like that person today, in one small way. then do it again tomorrow.
