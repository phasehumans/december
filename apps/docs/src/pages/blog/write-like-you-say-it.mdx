most people change when they start writing. they talk normally all day, then they open an email and become someone else. "please find attached." "kindly do the needful." "we are pleased to inform you." nobody talks like that. it's harder to read, the reader drifts off, and worst of all it fools the writer. big words make you feel like you said something, even when you didn't.

the fix is one question for every sentence: would i say this to a friend? if not, say what you'd actually say, and write that down. hard ideas don't need hard sentences. the people who know a subject best usually explain it the most plainly. and if a draft is too far gone to fix line by line, close it, explain the idea out loud to someone, and write down what you said.
