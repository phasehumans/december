## Automatic Restore Points

Every time December edits your files, it creates a safe checkpoint:

- **Automatic Checkpoints**: Every step saves a snapshot of your files before and after changes.
- **Review Diffs**: See exact line-by-line diffs so you always know what changed.
- **One-Command Undo**: Type `/undo` to instantly roll back if a change breaks your build or tests.
- **Clean Git State**: December respects your `.gitignore` and never touches ignored or private files.
